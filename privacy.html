Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

Hawkview respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Hawkview games and applications, including Don't Tap Red.

Information We Collect

Don't Tap Red does not collect, transmit, or share personal information.

The game does not require an account or registration and does not request access to contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, or other sensitive device information.

Information Stored on Your Device

Don't Tap Red stores your best game score locally on your device so that your high score can be displayed when you play again.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to Hawkview.

Advertising

Don't Tap Red does not currently display advertisements and does not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers.

Third-Party Services

Don't Tap Red does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-collection services.

Children's Privacy

Don't Tap Red does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults. The game does not require users to provide personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the features or services provided by Hawkview change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Hawkview applications, you can contact us through the contact information provided on the relevant Hawkview application or store listing.
